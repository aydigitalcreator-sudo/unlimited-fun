UNLIMITED FUN: Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: October 2026. Contact: aydigitalcreator@gmail.com

What we store

Your favorites, streak, XP, settings and choices are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. We do not run user accounts. Clearing browser data or changing device erases it.

AI features

Most features (chat, comebacks, love and friendship tests, scripts, trivia, daily message) are written by an AI. What you type, including any names you enter, is sent through our server to an AI provider (Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic, depending on our setup) to produce the result. Do not enter passwords, ID numbers or other sensitive details. We do not build a profile of you. Your favorites, streak and settings stay on your device.

Ads and cookies

We show ads using Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads. You can manage ad personalization in Google ad settings. See how Google uses data.

Sharing

When you share a result, your device's share options are used. We do not receive what you share.

Entertainment only

Love tests, fortunes, predictions and AI answers are for fun and are not professional, medical, legal or financial advice.

Acceptable use

Do not use the app to harass, threaten or harm others. We may limit or block abusive use.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.
