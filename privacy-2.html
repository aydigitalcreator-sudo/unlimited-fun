UNLIMITED FUN: Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: October 2026. Contact: aydigitalcreator@gmail.com

What we store

Your favorites, streak, XP, settings, chat history and AI friend notes are saved on your own device. We do not run user accounts and we do not keep your chats on our side. Clearing your browser data or changing device erases them. Chats and creations (like pictures and videos you make) are deleted automatically after 7 days. You can also delete them any time in Settings or My Stuff.

AI features

Chat, comebacks, love and friendship tests, scripts, video plans, trivia and the daily message are written by an AI. What you type is sent securely to an AI service provider (such as Google) to produce the reply. If you leave “Let my AI friend remember me” on, small facts you share (like your name or goals) are kept on your device and sent with your chats so replies feel personal. You can view, delete or turn this off in Settings. Do not type passwords, ID or bank details.

Images and videos

AI images are made by a free third-party image service from the words you type. The Video Maker works on your device and your photos are not uploaded.

Ads and cookies

This site shows Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to show and measure ads. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and manage ads at adssettings.google.com.

Staying safe

We will never ask you for money, passwords, PINs, OTP codes or bank details. Be careful of anyone who pretends to be us.

Entertainment only

Love tests, fortunes, predictions and AI answers are for fun and are not professional, medical, legal or financial advice.

Acceptable use

Do not use the app to harass, threaten, scam or harm others, or to create fake or misleading messages. We may limit or block abusive use.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.
